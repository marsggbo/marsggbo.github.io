I am currently an AI Engineer at Baidu Singapore. Prior to this, I was a Research Scientist at [A\*STAR CFAR](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/cfar), Singapore, working with [Prof. Ong Yew Soon](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=h9oWOsEAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Ivor W. Tsang](https://scholar.google.com.sg/citations?user=rJMOlVsAAAAJ&hl=en). I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), advised by [Prof. Chu Xiaowen](https://sites.google.com/view/chuxiaowen). I earned my Bachelor degree from the School of Electronic Information and Communications at Huazhong University of Science & Technology (HUST).

My research focuses on making large-scale AI models efficient, deployable, and trustworthy.
