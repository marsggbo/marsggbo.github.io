> 若随机变量$X$服从二项分布,即$X\sim B(n,p)$， 则有$P(X=k)=C_n^kp^k(1-p)^{n-k}$，其均值和方差分别是 $E(X)=np$ $D(X)=np(1-p)$

之前学二项分布的时候看到它的期望和方差觉得形式很简单，就没怎么细看推导过程。但是自己去推导的时候发现也没那么简单。。。本文做个总结

## **二项分布期望**

整个推导过程如下

$$
\begin{align} E(k) &=\sum_{k=0}^{n} k p(k)  \\   &=\sum_{k=0}^{n} k\left(\begin{array}{l} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k\left(\begin{array}{l} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k \frac{n !}{k !(n-k) !} p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k \frac{n !}{k !(n-k) !} p^{k} q^{n-k} \\ &=n p \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{(n-1) !}{(k-1) !(n-k) !} p^{k-1} q^{(\mathrm{n}-1)-(\mathrm{k}-1)} \\ &=n p  \end{align} \\
$$

* 第1到第3行应该很好理解，不过需要注意的是第3行的下标从 $k=1$开始了，因为$k=0$时值为0所以省略了。
* 第4行：把排列组合展开了
* 第5行：令$q=1-p$
* 第6行：这是整个推导过程magic所在。为更加方便理解，对于式(6)右边那一坨 $\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}p^{k-1}q^{(n-1)-(k-1)}$，我们可以做一下换元，即令$z=k-1,m=n-1$，注意原式的k取值范围是![k\in[1,n]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k%5Cin%5B1%2Cn%5D),那么z的取值范围应该就是![z\in[0,n-1]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%5Cin%5B0%2Cn-1%5D)，所以换元后式(6)变形得到$\sum_{z=0}^{m}\frac{m!}{z!(m-z)!}p^zq^{m-z}$,这就是二项分布概率累加，其结果为1。

## **二项分布方差**

![D(X)=E[X-EX]^2=E[X^2-2XEX+(EX^2)]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=D%28X%29%3DE%5BX-EX%5D%5E2%3DE%5BX%5E2-2XEX%2B%28EX%5E2%29%5D)

注意$EX$可视为一个常数，所以![E[2XEX]=2EX E[X]=2(EX)^2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=E%5B2XEX%5D%3D2EX+E%5BX%5D%3D2%28EX%29%5E2)，同理![E[(EX)^2]=(EX)^2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=E%5B%28EX%29%5E2%5D%3D%28EX%29%5E2)，综上 $D(X)=EX^2-(EX)^2$

下面我们只需要在计算$EX^2$即可，推导过程和上面比较类似，具体如下：

![\begin{align} E\left(k^{2}\right) &=\sum_{k=0}^{n} k^{2} p(k) \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{2}\left(\begin{array}{l} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k} q^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n}[\mathrm{k}(\mathrm{k}-1)+\mathrm{k}]\left(\begin{array}{c} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k} q^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k(k-1)\left(\begin{array}{c} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k} q^{n-k}+\sum_{k=1}^{n} k\left(\begin{array}{c} n \\ k \end{array}\right) p^{k} q^{n-k} \\ &=\sum_{k=1}^{n} k(k-1) \frac{n !}{k !(n-k) !} p^{k} q^{n-k}+n p \\ &=n(n-1) p^{2} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{(n-2) !}{(k-2) !(n-k) !} p^{k-2} q^{(n-2)-(k-2)}+n p \\ &=n(n-1) p^{2}+n p \end{align} \\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+E%5Cleft%28k%5E%7B2%7D%5Cright%29+%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+k%5E%7B2%7D+p%28k%29+%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+k%5E%7B2%7D%5Cleft%28%5Cbegin%7Barray%7D%7Bl%7D+n+%5C%5C+k+%5Cend%7Barray%7D%5Cright%29+p%5E%7Bk%7D+q%5E%7Bn-k%7D+%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D%5B%5Cmathrm%7Bk%7D%28%5Cmathrm%7Bk%7D-1%29%2B%5Cmathrm%7Bk%7D%5D%5Cleft%28%5Cbegin%7Barray%7D%7Bc%7D+n+%5C%5C+k+%5Cend%7Barray%7D%5Cright%29+p%5E%7Bk%7D+q%5E%7Bn-k%7D+%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+k%28k-1%29%5Cleft%28%5Cbegin%7Barray%7D%7Bc%7D+n+%5C%5C+k+%5Cend%7Barray%7D%5Cright%29+p%5E%7Bk%7D+q%5E%7Bn-k%7D%2B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+k%5Cleft%28%5Cbegin%7Barray%7D%7Bc%7D+n+%5C%5C+k+%5Cend%7Barray%7D%5Cright%29+p%5E%7Bk%7D+q%5E%7Bn-k%7D+%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+k%28k-1%29+%5Cfrac%7Bn+%21%7D%7Bk+%21%28n-k%29+%21%7D+p%5E%7Bk%7D+q%5E%7Bn-k%7D%2Bn+p+%5C%5C+%26%3Dn%28n-1%29+p%5E%7B2%7D+%5Csum_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7Bn%7D+%5Cfrac%7B%28n-2%29+%21%7D%7B%28k-2%29+%21%28n-k%29+%21%7D+p%5E%7Bk-2%7D+q%5E%7B%28n-2%29-%28k-2%29%7D%2Bn+p+%5C%5C+%26%3Dn%28n-1%29+p%5E%7B2%7D%2Bn+p+%5Cend%7Balign%7D+%5C%5C)
